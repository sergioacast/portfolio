Ballon d'Or Predictor

Rendered notebooks · Project README · 2026 prediction · All sports analytics

The 2026 prediction is not scored yet. The 2026 winner has not been announced, so there is no result to compare it with. The accuracy figures below come from past seasons (2010–2025) only. See the 2026 prediction.

Question

Can player stats and team success predict who wins the Ballon d'Or?

Data

  • Nominees and final ranks: Wikipedia, 2010–2025 (no 2020 edition). The 2026 nominees come from a hand-made CSV because there are no ranks yet.
  • Player stats: FBref (Big 5 European leagues), pulled with soccerdata.
  • Hand-made tables: team success (league title and Champions League stage), international tournament stage, and name and club fixes.
  • Size: 408 labelled nominee rows (15 winners) plus 30 nominees for 2026.

FBref and Wikipedia remain the property of their owners; this project is for learning and portfolio use. The raw data, loader scripts and SQL schema are not published here; the notebooks are shown as saved.

Method

Tools: Python, pandas, scikit-learn, SQLAlchemy, PostgreSQL, soccerdata, Jupyter

  • The data is loaded into a PostgreSQL database. The ballon_dor_features view has one row per nominee per season: 408 labelled rows plus 30 for 2026.
  • Features (from the notes): goals, assists, minutes, Champions League stage (0–6), international tournament stage (0–6), league title, a has_stats flag and the main position. Nominees outside the Big 5 get has_stats = 0 and median-filled values.
  • With only 15 winners in 408 rows, accuracy would be misleading. Evaluation is leave-one-season-out: train on 14 seasons, predict the 15th, and count how often the real winner is ranked first (top 1) or in the top 3.
  • Three models: a baseline (the player with the most goals), logistic regression (scaled, balanced) and a random forest (200 trees, balanced).

Misses and limitations

  • Misses: 2012 (Messi), 2018 (Modrić) and 2024 (Rodri) were never in the top 3. Voter narrative and star power are not in the features.
  • 2025 (Dembélé) changes with the seed, so it is not a stable prediction. 2021 and 2023 (Messi) are the "best stats vs. voters" cases.
  • Small sample: 15 winners.
  • No voter or narrative features (for example news or social sentiment); a possible v2.
  • Stats cover Big 5 league players only. The 12 nominees outside them (Santos, Ajax, Benfica, Sporting) have no FBref stats, and 3 of the 30 nominees for 2026 (Messi, Mané, Quiñones) are in the same position.
  • No xG or xA: FBref via soccerdata no longer provides them.
